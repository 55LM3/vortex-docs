## Summary

> [!NOTE]
> `RunService` is available through `game:GetService("RunService")` in both
> `Script` and `LocalScript`.

Runtime probes confirmed the service exists. They did not expose any readable
properties, callable methods, or connectable signals in the tested Vortex
runtime.
